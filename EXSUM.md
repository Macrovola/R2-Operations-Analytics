# Executive Summary — R2 PMO Operations Analytics

**Handling:** UNCLASSIFIED // Portfolio · Aggregate roll-up figures · Personnel and task-order identifiers sanitized

## Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF)

An R2 PMO operations-analyst function that chains four travel and supply trackers (MTT, local travel, site visits, SOA) into a single auditable YTD roll-up — sustaining continuous burn-rate visibility against a **$2.72M** FY26 ODC ceiling across **32 AOR sites**, converting a **$55.5K** training event into a documented **23.7% underrun**, and modernizing PMO reporting through automated A&L briefs and a revamped, 32-site supply-order SOP.

## Verdict

Fiscal stewardship is demonstrated and disciplined. The CST26 event landed **23.7% under authorization** at **$27.78 per cadet**, and the $2.72M ODC ceiling is governed by an auditable eight-step cycle holding **83.2% runway**. The open risk is *effectiveness*: utilization and outcome metrics are not yet captured, so return on the discretionary tail is asserted, not yet proven.

## 1. Facts

- **Four chained trackers** — MTT, local travel, management site visits, and SOA — each reconciling projected vs. incurred cost and rolling up (via chained Excel formulas) into one YTD Budget Tracker that feeds the TOSR and A&L briefs.
- **Reporting modernized** — A&L briefs reconstructed for PCM usability and automated (with J. Choun); the SOA SOP was revamped and issued to all 32 AOR sites, backed by a MS Teams community folder with navigation guidance.
- 44-day event (11 Jun – 24 Jul 2026); **191 PE-training-days** across **8 deployments**; **~2,000 cadets**.
- **$55,554.66** incurred = **76.3%** of the **$72,821.29** authorization → **23.7% underrun** ($17,266.63).
- Median **$194.42 / training day**; **~$27.78 / cadet**; mean **$6,944.33 / deployment**.
- Spend mix: **67.6% fixed** (per diem + lodging) / **32.4% discretionary** (travel tail).
- FY26 ODC ceiling **$2,715,719.20**; committed **$468,618.76**; **83.2%** remaining.

## 2. Interpretation

Cost is governed by travel logistics, not daily rates, and came in well under authorization — fiscally efficient. Procurement is defensible by design: segregation of duties (originator ≠ reviewer ≠ authorizer), dual authorization (APM/PM + COR-RA), a +10% cost buffer, a three-quote threshold above $3,500, and a single roll-up pipeline (SOA form → PERFAC → Option Year 1 tracker → YTD/by-location → TOSR & A&L briefs).

## 3. Recommendation

Accept the spend as justified. Next cycle:

1. Pair the fiscal roll-up with **utilization and outcome metrics** to convert an efficiency argument into an effectiveness one.
2. Bank savings from the **32.4% controllable tail** via rental pooling, fly-vs-drive discipline, advance airfare (≥14 days), and single-packet control.
3. Standardize the expense-report format and enforce task-order and date-match gates at intake (this cycle caught a wrong task-order citation and an uncountersigned report).
