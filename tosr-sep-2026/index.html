Assumptions & limits
  • Figures come from the seven-slide enclosure only. They have not been checked against CostPoint, the general ledger or vendor receipts.
  • The OY1/OY2 split by location is read from the row shading on Slide 5 (green = OY1, peach = OY2). Both groups tie exactly to the stated option-year totals, but the slide has no legend confirming the meaning.
  • Delivery and receipt status for the September orders is not in the source data.
  • Assigning the $6,383.74 (MTT) and $4,487.57 (Other SOA) depends on travel dates and vendor records not yet reviewed.
  • The corrected OY2 balance assumes spent, pending and projected costs are all deducted. The scenario builder shows other choices.

Section 1 · OY1 financial status at close (16 SEP 26)

  • September (1–16 SEP 26). OY1 incurred $162,691.41: MTT $7,235.75, site visits $579.21, local miles $453.57 and supply orders $154,422.88 (Amazon bulk order, 14–16 SEP). Projected MTT is $16,028.86.
  • OY1 overall. Against the $2,715,719.20 FY26 ODC budget, OY1 spent $790,597.57 (29.1%). Travel accounts for $510,976.19 (MTT $443,007.66, site visits $59,436.99, local miles $8,531.54). Supply orders account for $279,621.38 (Staples and Amazon $251,361.85, other SOAs $28,259.53). Pending SOAs total $8,601.13.
  • Uncommitted funds. The reported balance is $1,361,969.28 (50.2%). Depending on the calculation method, the balance ranges from $1,159,277.27 (42.7%) to $1,925,121.63 (70.9%); see Section 3.

Section 2 · OY2 financial actions, 17 SEP 26 to present

  • Budget. OY2 began 17 SEP 26 with an FY27 ODC budget of $1,439,331.60.
  • Incurred (17–30 SEP 26). $56,601.40: Amazon bulk order $52,108.41 (24–26 SEP), Staples $3,854.66 and local miles $638.33.
  • Committed, not yet spent. October SOA $81,268.60 (pending) and MTT $10,427.19 (projected).
  • Running estimate. Uncommitted funds are $1,291,034.41 (89.7%) when spent, pending and projected costs are all deducted. This replaces the reported $1,353,116.44 (94.0%).

Section 3 · Context

  • Purpose. This section reconciles the figures in Sections 1 and 2 and sets financial priorities for October 2026. Option Year 1 (OY1) closed on 16 SEP 26 and Option Year 2 (OY2) began on 17 SEP 26, so September activity is separated at that date. The separation gives OY2 a baseline that can be verified.
  • OY1 close (1–16 SEP 26). OY1 incurred $162,691.41 in September, leaving $1,361,969.28 (50.2%) uncommitted. The OY1 Total row still showed full-month figures from before the 16 SEP split. Recalculated, it is $790,597.57 spent and $765,844.36 projected.
  • Unassigned September costs. Comparing the full-month September view with the two option-year views shows $10,871.31 assigned to neither option year ($6,383.74 MTT, $4,487.57 Other SOA). Each cost will be assigned to OY1 or OY2 according to whether it was incurred before or after 17 SEP 26.
  • Purchase activity. The $206,531.29 Amazon bulk order (37 sites) is split between the option years by order date. Orders placed 14–16 SEP ($154,422.88) are OY1 costs, and orders placed 24–26 SEP ($52,108.41) are OY2 costs. Total cost came in $84,046.51 (28.9%) under the $290,577.80 projection.
  • OY2 baseline (17–30 SEP 26). The OY2 tracker lists the $52,108.41 OY2 Amazon purchase as pending rather than incurred. It also counts the OY1 local miles twice and counts the $81,268.60 October SOA as incurred in September. With these items corrected, OY2 uncommitted funds are $1,291,034.41 (89.7%), compared with the reported $1,353,116.44 (94.0%). The reported figure is about $62K too high.
  • Method risk. The uncommitted-funds balance was not calculated the same way every month: some months deduct only spent, while others also deduct projected. As a result, the balance cannot be reproduced from the table. Adopting one method for OY2 is recommended so that the percentage of funds remaining reported to the government is reliable.
  • October priorities.
    1. Adopt one method for calculating uncommitted funds.
    2. Assign the $10,871.31 to an option year using the 17 SEP 26 start date.
    3. Restate the OY2 baseline.
    4. Close out the $8,601.13 in open OY1 SOAs.
    5. Track the $81,268.60 October SOA and the $10,427.19 in projected MTT through to incurred in CostPoint.
  • Bottom line. Line by line, the OY1 figures hold up. The errors are concentrated in how September was split at the 16/17 SEP boundary, and they appear to be categorization errors rather than missing funds. Once they are corrected, OY2 begins 1QFY27 from a baseline that can be verified.
